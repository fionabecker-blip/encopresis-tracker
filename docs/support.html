Where is my data?

All entries are stored on your device only. There is no account and no server. This also means data does not transfer between devices, and deleting the app permanently deletes all entries. Export a report before deleting or changing devices if you want to keep a record.

Exporting a report

On the Progress tab, use "Export report" to generate a PDF covering the last 30 days. You can email it, save it, or print it for an appointment.

Medical questions

This app is a logging tool and cannot answer questions about your child's care. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Please contact your child's doctor.
